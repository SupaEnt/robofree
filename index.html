MEET THE VISIONARY

Hi, I’m Bea.
I started this because I missed being human.

After 15 years building tech empires, I walked away from the screens and the algorithms. I wanted my children to know the sound of birds instead of notifications. I wanted to remember what my own hands could create.

RoboFree is my love letter to humanity. If you feel the same quiet ache for something real — welcome home.
